# Foundation Screen Specification

## Shared visual system
- Warm ivory canvas with white cards in light mode.
- Near-black canvas and charcoal surfaces in dark mode.
- Coral, blue, lime, and amber are accent colours; use them purposefully.
- Editorial serif headings paired with a clean sans-serif body.
- 8dp spacing rhythm, approximately 20dp horizontal page padding.
- Cards: 16–22dp corner radius.
- Primary buttons: 52–56dp height.
- Interactive touch targets: minimum 48dp.
- Use large, high-quality product imagery and restrained elevation.
- Keep transitions smooth and purposeful; avoid decorative motion that slows workflows.

## Screen 1 — Splash / onboarding
- Aura Couture identity and logo/wordmark.
- Brief value proposition: boutique shopping and intelligent styling.
- Clear entry actions for sign in and account creation where appropriate.
- Avoid delaying returning users with unnecessary onboarding.

## Screen 2 — Login
- Email and password fields.
- Sign-in action and password recovery entry.
- Customer registration path.
- Employee access is controlled; users cannot select or grant themselves employee privileges.
- Clear validation and error states.

## Screen 3 — Employee dashboard
- Greeting and concise business overview.
- Quick actions: add/scan product, view products, create order.
- Useful summary metrics such as product count, low-stock count, and recent orders.
- AI insight area only when real authorised data is available.
- Employee bottom navigation proposal: Home, Products, Orders, AI Studio.

## Screen 4 — Product catalogue
- Product image, name, category, price, and stock indicator.
- Search and useful category/availability filters.
- Product detail and edit entry points for authorised employees.
- Clear empty, loading, and error states.
- Add-product action.

## Screen 5 — AI Scan to Stock
- Capture with camera or choose an image.
- Show scan progress and a clear retry path.
- Display AI suggestions for category, type, colour, pattern, material, style, description, and tags.
- All AI-suggested fields remain editable and require employee review.
- Employee manually enters business-critical values: selling price, cost price, sizes, colours, and quantities.
- Save only after required fields are validated.
- Provide a manual-entry fallback if AI is unavailable.

## Later screens
- Product details and manual add/edit product.
- Customer management.
- Create order, order list, and order details/history.
- Customer browse, product details, bag, and order history.
- Customer AI Scan to Style.
- AI Studio and profile/settings/theme.

## Navigation proposal
Employee: Home → Products → Orders → AI Studio.  
Customer: Browse → Style AI → Bag → Orders/Profile.

Navigation and role access must be consistent with the final coursework requirements and security rules.
